# Student AI Product Synthesis

Student AI is not just a school chatbot. It is a guided learning workspace that combines explanation, revision, presentation prep and project framing.

The differentiation is the bridge with Pixelrises builders:
- a student can revise a topic
- prepare a presentation
- then transform an idea into a site, app, game or agent brief

This keeps Student AI useful now in V2 while staying extensible for later phases.
